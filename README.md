# Center for Open Science Advocacy to Policy Makers
The Center for Open Science (COS) advocates for open science policies to policymakers in government and private organizations. This includes Congressional testimony, responses to Requests for Information (RFIs), policy briefs to decisionmakers on key topics, and other formats. These statements are publicly available and are openly licensed for maximal reuse by others.

The key topics we advocate for are:
* Effective adoption and implementation of the <strong>Transparency and Openness Promotion (TOP) Guidelines</strong> by journals, funders, and research-performing institutions
* <strong>Researcher assessment reform</strong> to appropriately recognize and reward practices that promote the openness, integrity, and trustworthiness of research
* <strong>Sustainable resourcing for open research infrastructure</strong> that enable and facilitate appropriate sharing of research plans, outputs, and outcomes that enable their discovery and reuse
